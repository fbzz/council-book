# INC-0003 · Model-provider billing outage: scheduled runs missed

On 29 Sep 2026 the model provider refused calls over a billing problem on the account. Scheduled paper slots that day did not run: no council met and nothing was decided or published for them. Since 1 Oct a refused call of this kind stops the run at once with a billing-error flag and alerts the operator, instead of waiting through the outage retries.
