# INC-0003 · Model-provider billing outage: one run without replies, one run late

On 29 Sep 2026 the model provider refused every call over a billing problem on the account (seen at about 18:05 UTC). A paper run of the 14:40 UTC slot got no model replies: no idea was reviewed and nothing was decided. The account was fixed by about 19:50 UTC and the 18:40 UTC slot then ran 69 minutes late; no other slot was affected. Since 1 Oct a refused call of this kind stops the run at once with a billing-error flag and alerts the operator, instead of waiting through the outage retries.
