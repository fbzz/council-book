# INC-0002 · The Skeptic's model swapped from GLM to DeepSeek

The Skeptic ran on GLM, a different model family from the Scout, so that it would not share the Scout's blind spots. On the early paper test runs GLM returned a usable reply on only 1 of 3 Skeptic calls (none on the run before): it ignored the structured-output and no-reasoning settings and spent its budget writing prose. On 29 Sep 2026 the Skeptic moved to the Scout's model family (DeepSeek). It still judges blind (claim, catalyst and fact card, never the Scout's thesis), but its independence now rests on its inputs, not on a different model; every run carries a 'same model' flag.
