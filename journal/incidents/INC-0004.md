# INC-0004 · Data and model outage, then two slots not run

At the 30 Sep 2026 14:40 UTC paper slot the model provider, the price feed and the SEC feed were all unreachable: the Scout could not run, the movers screen was missing, and the cycle closed with nothing proposed. The 30 Sep 18:40 UTC slot was then not run at all, and the 1 Oct 14:40 UTC slot ran 83 minutes late, because the paper scheduler lived inside an interactive session that was busy at those times. A standalone scheduled job for paper runs now exists (operator-loaded); until it is loaded, a busy session can still miss a slot.
